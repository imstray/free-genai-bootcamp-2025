## ChatGPT Teaching Assistant

Mostly conducted across GPT 4o-mini but the ocassional testing conducted on o3-mini

Found that o3-mini performed better and was more capable of following the details of the prompt given. However, this comes at a cost of time where it takes a significantly longer period to produce this better result.

4o-mini/4o (during the free allowance) still managed to perform well but produced more lapses of judgement on a more frequent basis than o3-mini. 
Sometimes it would struggle with gameified aspects such as taking a point off for incorrect answers. More often than not the issue was with dealing in single word guidance and so up to the preference by the user of what kind of teaching style would be preferred. i tried to have the assistant not provide solutions even for single word entries and instead try to provide multiple options or even add a limit for incorrect responses before the teaching assistant would 'step in' and just provide the answer for the context.


i wanted to put my own spin on teaching style so tried to encourage the assistant to focus on smaller parts of the sentence and try to ask questions to the student about smaller parts before constructing
the entire sentence with the parts.

also hoped to introduce some game elements by having rewards to the student. this is something that was more effective on reasoning models. I did not do enough extensive research into other AI models but 
I did play slightly with Perplexity's offering of DeepSeek R1 which again as a reasoning model did in fact perform better especially for keeping scores. It was also more capable of building tables with
multiple options and providing the user with potential answers to see if the student could pick the correct word. 

understand that my approach be different to what was intended but i guess that is my perspective on this challenge and found it interesting to play around with this prompt engineering as it is an 
interesting art form to play with.
